Maya found a brass key beneath the last seat on the midnight train.
A tiny label tied to it read, "For the door that appears only in the rain."
When she stepped onto the empty platform, thunder rolled over the station.
At the end of the tracks, a blue door flickered into view.

Maya turned the brass key and stepped into a station filled with sunlight.
Her grandfather, missing for ten years, stood beside a clock that had stopped at midnight.
He explained that the door could carry them home, but only before the rain ended.
Maya took his hand and pulled him through as the final drops struck the platform.
The blue door vanished, leaving them together beneath the first clear sky of morning.
