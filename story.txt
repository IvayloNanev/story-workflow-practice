Maya found a brass key beneath the last seat on the midnight train.
A tiny label tied to it read, "For the door that appears only in the rain."
When she stepped onto the empty platform, thunder rolled over the station.
At the end of the tracks, a blue door flickered into view.

Maya turned the brass key, and the blue door opened onto a sunlit room filled with hundreds of ticking clocks.
The smallest clock chimed once, painting the door bright orange and freezing the rain in midair.
Among the clocks stood her grandfather, missing for ten years, beside a clock stopped at midnight.
He explained that the door could carry them home, but only before time and the rain began again.
Maya took his hand and crossed the threshold; behind them, the door vanished into a clear morning while the key pointed toward their next mystery.
