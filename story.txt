Maya found a brass key beneath the last seat on the midnight train.
A tiny label tied to it read, "For the door that appears only in the rain."
When she stepped onto the empty platform, thunder rolled over the station.
At the end of the tracks, a blue door flickered into view.
Maya slipped the key into its lock, but before she could turn it, the blue paint began to glow.
It swirled like sunset clouds until the entire door had changed to a brilliant orange.
When the door opened, warm morning light spilled across the rainy platform, revealing a path that led Maya safely home.
