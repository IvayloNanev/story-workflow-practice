Maya found a brass key beneath the last seat on the midnight train.
A tiny label tied to it read, "For the door that appears only in the rain."
When she stepped onto the empty platform, thunder rolled over the station.
At the end of the tracks, a blue door flickered into view.
Maya turned the brass key, and the blue door swung open onto a room filled with hundreds of ticking clocks.
The smallest clock chimed once, painting the door bright orange and freezing the rain in midair.
Maya stepped through before time could begin again, carrying the key toward whatever mystery waited beyond the next door.
